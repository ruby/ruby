/(?<n>a)\k<>/
           ^ group name is empty: /(?<n>a)\k<>/
/(?<n>a)\k<n-x>/
           ^~~~ invalid group name <n-x>>: /(?<n>a)\k<n-x>/
/(a)\k<1a>/
       ^~ invalid group name <1a>: /(a)\k<1a>/
/(a)\k<0>/
       ^~ invalid group name <0>>: /(a)\k<0>/
/(a)\g<1-x>/
       ^~~~ invalid char in group name <1-x>>: /(a)\g<1-x>/
/(a)\g<->/
       ^~ invalid group name <->>: /(a)\g<->/
/(?<n>a)\k<n+99999999999>/
           ^~~~~~~~~~~ too big number: /(?<n>a)\k<n+99999999999>/
/(?<n>a)(?(<n>b)/
              ^ undefined group option: /(?<n>a)(?(<n>b)/
